Owie XIAO ESP32-S3 Dual RS485 Wiring

Diagram

Uses two 3.3V SP3485 RS485 breakout boards, one permanently enabled as
receiver on the BMS-side pair and one permanently enabled as transmitter on
the controller-side pair. Assumes OneWheel white is RS485 A/non-inverting
and green is RS485 B/inverting; swap A/B on a pair if the logic analyzer
shows inverted or undecodable UART data.
